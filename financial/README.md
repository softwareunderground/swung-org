## Phase 1: Get the Ball Rolling & Data Handoff
*(Timeline: Roughly January – Early June)*

| # | Action | Who Does It | Key Notes / What to Look For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1.** | **The Official Heads-Up** | FO | **Start Proactively in the New Year (Jan/Feb) OR when you receive the Annual Return Notice, whichever is sooner.** This is your trigger to start the year-end close. |
| **2.** | **Start the Bookkeeper** | FO | Send a quick email to the **Bookkeeper (BK)** to tell them it's time to start close of the books for the year. |
| **3.** | **Collect Expense Receipts** | FO to Board | **Reach out to all board members** to collect any outstanding receipts or expense forms needed for reconciliation. |
| **4.** | **Send Source Documents** | FO to BK | **Access bank and VISA accounts** to download and send the complete year's statements to the **BK**. Be ready to send additional statements upon request later on. |
| **5.** | **BK Does Their Thing** | BK | The **BK prepares** the necessary year-end reports (P&L, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance) and makes sure all expense reports are reconciled. |
| **6.** | **Data is Sent!** | BK to AC | **Crucial Step:** The **BK sends those reports directly to the Tax Accountant (AC)**. You should be copied on this email—that's how you confirm it happened. |

---

## Phase 2: FO Oversight & Wrapping Up
*(Timeline: Roughly Mid-June)*

| # | Action | Who Does It | Key Notes / What to Look For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **7.** | **Check In** | FO | **Follow up** with the **BK** and/or the **AC** to make sure they got the documents and to check on their progress. Don't assume everything's fine! |
| **8.** | **Final Details** | FO | The **AC** may email asking for something simple for the tax form, like current contact number, or other. **Reply fast.** |
| **9.** | **AGM Report Review** | FO | Look over the finalized financial reports from the **AC/BK**. **Sanity check the expenses** (e.g., make sure accounting fees are actually listed). Then, pull the numbers for the AGM summary, prepare slides. |

*(Timeline: Whenever the Invoice Hits)*

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
