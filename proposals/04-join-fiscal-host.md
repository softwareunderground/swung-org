# Proposal: Join a fiscal host

Authors:

* Matt Hall
* Dieter Werthmüller
* Santi Soler
  
## Description

We propose to join **Open Source Europe**, a [fiscal host](https://opencollective.com/fiscal-hosting) based in Belgium. We would collect funds (e.g. donations or sales of merch) through this host, and seek reimbursment for our expenses through them.

As of 28 April 2026, Open Source Europe fiscally hosts 430 _collectives_. Software Underground would be another such collective — in fact we already exist in [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com).

Taking the action would sidestep the need to run a registered non-profit ad to have our own bank account, which both carry substantial overhead (see _Context_, below). The non-profit would be dissolved by a member vote (e.g. at the Annual General Meeting).

## Context

We formed the non-profit in 2020, partly to make fundraising and event finances more transparent, and partly to make Slack more affordable (there was a large discount for non-profits).

However, several changes in circumstances have made the overhead of maintaining the non-profit less appealing:

- Slack's pricing model changed, making non-profits much more expensive.
- Software Underground has run fewer events since Covid, and the online events we did run were much cheaper to host.

The overhead of running the non-profit includes:

- Having to have a board of directors that is liable for the organization's actions and finances.
- Having to maintain the organization's records with the Government of Canada (eg annual reports, changes to directors).
- Having to file taxes with the Canada Revenue Agency every year, and paying an accountant to do this.
- Having to maintain a bank account and credit card; the credit card locks up a CAD 2000 GIC as guarantee.

## Terms and costs

[The terms of the fiscal hosting are here.](https://docs.google.com/document/u/2/d/e/2PACX-1vQmcVk_7Q_QH0czqcs_Xrr1dRP7jEGqHF5tSrBWeRazBnJyVOCrECwkes7phE6xTw/pub)

The host charges an 8% hosting fee (deducted from donations). They also seek voluntary add-on donations through the funding process.

## Steps

1. Apply to join the fiscal host.
2. If accepted, switch donation channels, the shop, etc, to the fiscal host.
3. Complete our 2025 year end with the accountant, plus a closing account for 2026.
4. Clear any credit card balance and sell the GIC back to the bank.
5. Transfer remaining funds from our bank account to the host.
6. Close the bank account.
7. Close the organization with the Government of Canada and Canada Revenue Agency, via letter.

## Challenges

We do not foresee any undue challenges, the process seems quite straightforward.
